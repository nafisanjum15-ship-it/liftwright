Reporting something broken

Four things make a problem far quicker to fix, and you can find all of them in a minute:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version, from Settings → General → About.
  • The app version, from More → About → Version.
  • What you tapped, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if there is anything to see.

If the app has data you would rather not lose while a problem is being sorted out, export a backup first: More → Data → Export data writes a JSON file and a CSV you can keep anywhere.

Common questions

My workouts are not appearing on my other device

Check More → iCloud sync is on. Both devices have to be signed into the same Apple Account, and the setting is read when the app opens, so quit Liftwright fully and reopen it after changing it. A first sync can take a few minutes.

My weigh-ins are not syncing, but everything else is

That one is deliberate. A weigh-in can be read from Apple Health, and Health data is never placed in iCloud, so weigh-ins stay on the device that recorded them. To move them, use Export data and Restore from backup.

Workouts are not showing up in Apple Health

Health is off until you turn it on under More → Apple Health. If it is on and nothing is arriving, check iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Liftwright and make sure writing is allowed. Only workouts finished after you granted access are written.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Not in this version.

Does it run on iPad?

Liftwright is an iPhone app. It will run on an iPad in iPhone compatibility mode, but the layout is designed for a phone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

With iCloud sync on, sign into the same Apple Account on the new phone and your programs, workouts and records arrive on their own. Weigh-ins do not, for the reason above, so export a backup first if you want to keep them.

How do I delete everything?

Deleting the app removes what is stored on the iPhone. Anything in iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings, and anything written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.
