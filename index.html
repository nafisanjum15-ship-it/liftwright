Liftwright Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Liftwright ("the app") is a strength-training log for iPhone. This policy covers what the app stores and where that data goes. In brief, your data stays on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. We run no servers and do not track you. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send it.

What the app stores

The app keeps what you enter or generate while training:

  • Programs, workout days and the exercises in them, including exercises you add yourself.
  • Workout sessions: the sets, reps, weights, rest times and notes you log.
  • Personal records and progression suggestions calculated from those sessions.
  • Body-weight entries you log on the Weigh-In screen.
  • The name, age, gender and height you enter when you first set the app up. You can change any of them later on the Account screen.
  • Settings such as your weight unit, appearance and rest-timer preferences.

All of this lives on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, so none of it is sent to us.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default, under More → iCloud sync. While it is on, your programs, workouts and records are stored in the private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple Account. Apple stores that data, and neither we nor anyone else can read it. With sync off, everything stays on your iPhone. Body-weight entries never go to iCloud; the Apple Health section explains why.

You can delete the iCloud copy at any time from your iCloud storage settings in the iOS Settings app.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration stays off until you turn it on under More → Apple Health, and iOS asks for your permission when you do. With that permission the app:

  • Writes each finished workout to Health as a strength-training workout.
  • Writes the body-weight entries you log to Health as Body Mass.
  • Reads the most recent body-weight sample that another app or a scale wrote, so the Weigh-In screen can offer to log it with one tap.

The app uses Health data only to show and record your own weight and workouts. It does not use Health data for advertising or marketing, and it does not sell it or share it with anyone. The app also never stores Health data in iCloud, which is why body-weight entries stay on your iPhone only. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps & Services, and delete anything the app wrote from there as well.

Notifications, Live Activities and the widget

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local alert on your iPhone for when a rest timer ends. The only remote notifications the app receives are silent iCloud sync signals from Apple, and they carry no content. The lock-screen Live Activity and the home-screen widget show your current workout and the next day in your program, using data already on your iPhone.

Backups you export

Export data, under More → Data, creates a JSON backup and a CSV of your sessions and passes them to the iOS share sheet. You decide where they go from there. Restore from backup reads a JSON file you pick. The app never uploads either file.

Feedback you send

Report a bug and Request a feature, under More → Feedback, open a new message to us in your own mail app. The app itself sends nothing. The message sits in your mail app until you press send, and you can read, edit or delete any part of it before then.

Below the space where you write, the message already contains four lines about your setup, so we can match a report to the build and device it came from:

  • The app version and build number.
  • Your iOS version.
  • Your device model, as an identifier such as iPhone17,1.
  • Whether iCloud sync is on, off, or unavailable in that build.

The message includes nothing else: no training data and no profile details. If you send it, we keep the message and the address it came from so we can reply. Ask and we will delete it.

What the app does not do

  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • It contains no third-party code.
  • It makes no network connections of its own. It sends data only to Apple's iCloud, and only while sync is on. An exported backup or a feedback message leaves through another app, when you tell it to.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts or photos, and it reads no identifier unique to your iPhone. Your name, age, gender and height stay on the device. The only way any of that, or your email address, reaches us is in a message you write and send yourself.

If you have opted in under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share aggregate crash and usage statistics with us. Apple anonymises that data, and it contains none of your training data.

Deleting your data and your rights

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including your profile details. You can remove data in iCloud from your iCloud storage settings, and anything written to Apple Health from the Health app. The only personal data we can hold is an email you chose to send us and the address it came from. Write to us and we will delete it. Everything else stays on your own devices, so we have nothing more to access, correct, export or erase on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and setup will not accept an age below 13. We do not knowingly hold personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships in the app update that needs it, with a new effective date at the top. The version inside the app is always the one that applies to that build.

Contact

Email liftwrightapp@gmail.com with any question about this policy or about what the app does with your data. Your message goes to the person who builds Liftwright.
