Liftwright Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026

Liftwright ("the app") is a strength-training log for iPhone. This policy explains what the app stores, where that data goes, and what never happens to it. The short version: your data stays on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account. We run no servers, we collect nothing, and we do not track you.

What the app stores

The app keeps what you enter or generate while training:

  • Programs, workout days and the exercises in them, including exercises you add yourself.
  • Workout sessions: the sets, reps, weights, rest times and notes you log.
  • Personal records and progression suggestions calculated from those sessions.
  • Body-weight entries you log on the Weigh-In screen.
  • Settings such as your weight unit, appearance, rest-timer preferences and the display name you type on the Account screen.

All of this lives on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us. The app has no account system, no sign-in and no server of its own.

iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is on (it is on by default, under More → iCloud sync), your programs, workouts and records are stored in the private iCloud database that belongs to your Apple Account. Apple stores that data; we cannot read it, and no other person can see it. Turning sync off keeps everything on your iPhone. Body-weight entries are never placed in iCloud, for the reason given under Apple Health.

You can delete the iCloud copy at any time from your iCloud storage settings in the iOS Settings app.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is off until you turn it on under More → Apple Health, and iOS asks for your permission when you do. With that permission the app:

  • Writes each finished workout to Health as a strength-training workout.
  • Writes the body-weight entries you log to Health as Body Mass.
  • Reads the most recent body-weight sample that another app or a scale wrote, so the Weigh-In screen can offer to log it with one tap.

Health data is used only to show and record your own weight and workouts inside the app. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with anyone, and never stored in iCloud by the app: body-weight entries stay on your iPhone only. You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps & Services, and delete anything the app wrote from there as well.

Notifications, Live Activities and the widget

If you allow notifications, the app schedules alerts on your iPhone when a rest timer ends. These are local notifications created on the device. The only remote notifications the app receives are silent iCloud sync signals from Apple, which carry no content. The lock-screen Live Activity and the home-screen widget show your current workout and the next day in your program using data already on your iPhone.

Backups you export

Export data, under More → Data, writes a JSON backup and a CSV of your sessions and hands them to the iOS share sheet. Where they go from there is your choice. Restore from backup reads a JSON file you pick. The app never uploads either file anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • It contains no third-party code.
  • It makes no network connections of its own. The only data that leaves your iPhone goes to Apple's iCloud, and only while sync is on.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Apple may share crash and usage statistics with us in aggregate if you have opted in under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Apple anonymises that data, and it contains none of your training data.

Deleting your data and your rights

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Data in iCloud can be removed from your iCloud storage settings. Anything written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase on request; every control listed here is yours directly.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update that needs it and the effective date at the top moves. The version inside the app is always the one that applies to that build.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact listed on the app's App Store page.
